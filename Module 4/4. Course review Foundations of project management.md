## 1. What You Learned

### Project Management Fundamentals

- What **project management** is.
    
- The **role and value of a Project Manager (PM)**.
    
- How PMs contribute to successful project outcomes.
    

### Project Life Cycle

Learned the stages and tasks needed to manage a project **from start to finish**.

**Project Life Cycle:**

> Initiate → Plan → Execute → Close

### Project Management Methodologies

- Learned different project management methodologies.
    
- Learned that there is **no single method that works for every project**.
    
- The PM should choose a methodology based on the project's needs and circumstances.
    

### Team Relationships

- Simple connections and relationships within a team can contribute significantly to project success.
    
- Building relationships helps PMs collaborate and work effectively with others.
    

### Organizational Structure & Culture

- An organization's **structure and culture affect how a project is managed**.
    
- Understanding these factors helps PMs:
    
    - Communicate effectively.
        
    - Identify the right stakeholders.
        
    - Navigate decision-making.
        
    - Manage change.
        
    - Adapt their approach to the organization.
