# Welcome to the Google Project Management Certificate
## Getting Started
Let's start with simple exercise, think about different tasks that you've completed in your life:
- You planned your wedding
- You planned a birthday party
- Filled your annual tax returns
- Move one state to another state
- You're the family member somehow, who every year is tasked with organizing the annual family reunion

You have been developing all kinds of skills through these tasks that'll help you become a successful project manager for any organizing or your own business.
